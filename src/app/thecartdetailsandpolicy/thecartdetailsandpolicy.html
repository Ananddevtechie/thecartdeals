We aim to get every order to you safely and as quickly as possible.

Order processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days. Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays. We will contact you if an item needs extra time to prepare.

Delivery timelines

Most orders arrive within 3–7 business days after dispatch. Delivery to remote areas can take longer. Timelines are estimates and may be affected by weather, local restrictions, or courier delays.

Delivery charges and serviceability

Delivery is free where available. Serviceability and any location-specific charge are confirmed at checkout after you enter your PIN code.

Delivery assistance

Please make sure your phone number and delivery address are correct when placing your order. For help with a delayed delivery, message us with your order number.

We want you to be happy with your purchase. Here is how returns and refunds work.

Return window and eligibility

Contact us within 7 days of delivery to request a return. Items should be unused, unwashed, and returned with their original packaging, tags, and included accessories. Some products may not be returnable for hygiene or product-specific reasons; check the listing or contact us before ordering.

How to request a return

Message TheCart support on WhatsApp with your order number, the item you wish to return, and the reason. Our team will confirm eligibility and share the next steps. Please wait for return instructions before sending an item back.

Refunds

Once an approved return is received and checked, we will confirm the refund. Refunds for prepaid orders are sent to the original payment method; cash-on-delivery refunds are arranged with support. Please allow 5–7 business days after approval for the refund to appear, depending on your payment provider.

Damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the item and packaging so we can review and help.

Need to change your mind? These are the steps for cancelling an order.

Before dispatch

You may request cancellation while your order is still being prepared. Contact support as soon as possible with your order number. We will confirm whether the order can still be stopped.

After dispatch

Once an order has been handed to the courier, it can no longer be cancelled through our support team. If your item is eligible, you can contact us after delivery to request a return under the Return & Refund Policy.

Refund for a cancelled prepaid order

If we confirm cancellation of a prepaid order, the refund will be sent to the original payment method. It may take 5–7 business days to appear, depending on your payment provider.

Contact us

Message TheCart support on WhatsApp with your order number. Cancellation is complete only after we confirm it to you.

Information you provide

How information is used
